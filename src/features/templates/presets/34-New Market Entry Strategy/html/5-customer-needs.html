05 · Demand insight
Friction concentrates at purchase and onboarding
Journey mapped from 420 buyer interviews and 18 channel-partner sessions across the two priority segments.
01 Awareness
Category framed as commodity; brand trust built on word of mouth.
02 Evaluation
Specs compared informally; no trusted local benchmark exists.
03 Purchase
38% drop-off at checkout — financing and credit terms missing.
04 Onboarding
Setup takes 9 days; local-language support is thin.
05 Repeat
Repurchase driven by service response, not price promotions.
Unmet need 01
Financing at the point of sale
Only 2 of 6 competitors offer instalment terms; buyers cite cash flow as the top blocker.
Unmet need 02
Guaranteed service response
Average resolution runs 5.4 days. A 48-hour SLA is the single most valued attribute.
Unmet need 03
Locally relevant configuration
Imported specifications fit 64% of use cases; the remainder needs local adaptation.
