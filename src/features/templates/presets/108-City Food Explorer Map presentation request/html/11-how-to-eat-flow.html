P. 11
— CHAPTER NINE · THE PLAYBOOK
How to Eat a City.
Do these in sequence and the day builds. Do them out of order and you'll be full at the wrong moment.
STEP 01
Arrive
Hungry
Drop the bag. Skip the hotel breakfast entirely — you will need the room.
no airport sandwich
STEP 02
Caffeinate
Twice
One flat white at the harbour, one espresso standing in the old town.
never sit for coffee
STEP 03
Graze the
Market
Small plates, many vendors, no commitment. Eat standing. Tip the cook.
the pivot of the day
STEP 04
Sit Down
Properly
The 19:00 booking. One pasta, one house red, zero rush.
this is the anchor
STEP 05
Stay Up
Late
One cone by the river, one bowl downtown, then the last train.
dessert is not optional
OPTIONAL BRANCH · IF IT RAINS
Old Market Hall
Same grazing, under a roof. Rejoin the route at Step 04.
every good route
needs a wet-weather plan
THE ONE RULE
Never eat two sit-down meals in one day.
