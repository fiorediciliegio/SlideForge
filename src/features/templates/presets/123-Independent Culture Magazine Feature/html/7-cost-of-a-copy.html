Indicative model, rounded to the nearest 10p

Printing is the largest single line, and the one most people assume we could cut. We cannot. The second largest is contributor fees, and that is the one we refuse to cut.

What closes the gap is not efficiency. It is that no line in this model pays the editors anything at all.
