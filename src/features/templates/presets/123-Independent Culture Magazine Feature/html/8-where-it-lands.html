Retail looks like the serious channel because it is the visible one. In practice it is the slowest to pay and the first to return unsold stock.

Subscribers and fairs are the opposite: money arrives before the paper does.

That is the structural argument for staying small: a hand-sold run needs no warehouse, no sale-or-return terms, and no forecast.

The shelf is a shop window. The envelope is the business.
