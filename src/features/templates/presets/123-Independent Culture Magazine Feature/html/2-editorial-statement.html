We print
anyway.
★ Manifesto — read once, keep, ignore.

We started this magazine on a photocopier at three in the morning, and every issue since has tried to remember what that felt like.

We are not a platform. We are not a feed. We are a folded piece of paper with opinions, and we would like you to hold it.

Independent culture, in 2026, is not a niche — it is a discipline. It is the choice to make something slower than the internet, denser than the algorithm, and more specific than any brief.

This issue collects six voices we could not stop thinking about, and one photographer who kept the lens dirty on purpose.

“A magazine is a small, stubborn machine for slowing the world down long enough to look at it.”
— The Editors, 2026
