07  /  Engagement models

Three ways to buy us — one
underlying team

Most clients enter through a project, then convert to retainer once the platform is live. Embedded is reserved for in-house build-outs.

Model A

Project

A defined outcome with a fixed scope and end date — brand build, launch campaign or repositioning.

Commitment

8–14 weeks · from £65k

Best for: first collaboration, board-approved initiative.

Most common  ·  71% of revenue

Model B

Retainer

A standing senior team plus guaranteed studio capacity, planned quarter by quarter.

Commitment

12 months · £28k–£60k / month

Best for: always-on brands with a live channel plan.

Model C

Embedded

Our leads sit inside your marketing team to stand up or rebuild an in-house creative function.

Commitment

6 months · 2–3 days / week

Best for: scaling teams hiring their first creative lead.
