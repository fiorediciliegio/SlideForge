The first twelve weeks.
A patient build, one addition at a time — the only reliable way to know what works.
01–02
WEEKS · FOUNDATION
Three products only.
Cleanser, moisturizer, SPF. Establish a baseline before judging anything.
03–04
WEEKS · FIRST ACTIVE
Add one morning serum.
Vitamin C or niacinamide — the gentlest place to begin.
05–08
WEEKS · RENEWAL
Introduce retinol.
Twice weekly, buffered with moisturizer. Build frequency only if calm.
09–12
WEEKS · REFINE
Reassess, then hold.
Add exfoliation if needed. Then change nothing for a season.
— Skin keeps its own calendar. Twelve weeks is the shortest honest review. —
