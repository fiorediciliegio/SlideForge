THE ONE RULE
One new active at a time.
Introduce, observe for two weeks, then decide. Two at once tells you nothing.
Buffer, don't abandon.
If an active stings, apply moisturizer first — then the active on top.
Hydration never clashes.
Humectants and ceramides pair with everything, on every night.
Most irritation is a scheduling problem, not an ingredient problem.
