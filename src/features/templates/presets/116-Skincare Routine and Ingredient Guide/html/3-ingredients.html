The ingredients.
Six active molecules worth knowing — what they do, and where they belong in your rituals.
— The active edit —
i.
Hyaluronic Acid
Hydration
A humectant that binds up to 1,000× its weight in water. Applied to damp skin, it plumps and softens instantly.
ii.
Niacinamide
Balance
A form of vitamin B3 that refines pores, softens redness, and regulates oil. Well tolerated by nearly every skin.
iii.
Vitamin C
Radiance
An antioxidant that brightens tone, evens pigment, and shields against morning oxidative stress.
iv.
Retinol
Renewal
A vitamin A derivative that accelerates turnover. An evening ingredient — introduced slowly, always with SPF next day.
v.
Ceramides
Barrier
Lipids that reinforce the skin's natural barrier. Essential to any repair ritual, especially after actives.
vi.
AHA · BHA
Exfoliation
Chemical exfoliants that refine texture. AHAs address surface tone; BHAs decongest deeper into pores.
