When skin asks for more.
Situational care — how to adjust the ritual when weather, travel, or stress shift the balance.
I.
The Cold Months
Winter dryness
Layer a hydrating essence beneath a richer cream. Swap gel textures for balms. Add a humidifier and reduce actives to protect the barrier.
II.
The Warm Months
Heat & shine
Lighter fluids over creams. Incorporate niacinamide and a gentle BHA. Reapply SPF every two hours — the single habit that changes skin.
III.
The Unwelcome Guest
Breakouts
Spot-treat with salicylic acid; don't strip the whole face. Keep hydration — dry skin heals slower. Resist the urge to layer new products.
IV.
The Quiet Reset
Sensitivity flare
Pause all actives for a week. Return to cleanser, moisturizer, sunscreen only. Centella, panthenol, and rest do more than any new bottle.
The routine is a starting point — the adjustments are the art.
