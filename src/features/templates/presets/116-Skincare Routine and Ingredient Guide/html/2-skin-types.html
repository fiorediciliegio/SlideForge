Know your
skin.
Every routine begins with recognition — reading what your skin is asking for before deciding what to give it.
01
Dry
Tightness · Flaking · Dullness
Needs richer emollients, plant oils, and layered hydration. Favor ceramides, squalane, and shea to rebuild the barrier.
02
Oily
Shine · Congestion
Lightweight gels, gentle exfoliants, and non-comedogenic formulas. Niacinamide balances without stripping.
03
Combination
T-Zone · Dry Cheeks
A balanced approach — hydrating serums across the face, lighter textures over oil-prone zones only.
04
Sensitive
Redness · Reactivity
Minimal, fragrance-free formulas. Centella, panthenol, and oat calm; avoid harsh acids and retinoids.
