06 — Ways of working

Meeting and communication norms

Pick the channel before you pick the meeting — then follow the do-and-don't rules for mixed rooms.

Choose the lightest channel that works

Written doc

Decisions, proposals, status — default choice

Chat thread

Quick unblocks, answered within one working day

Video call

Debate, feedback, anything with real ambiguity

In person

Kick-offs, tough conversations, creative sessions

Four meeting rules, no exceptions

01

One person, one screen. If anyone is remote, everyone joins from their own device.

02

Agenda or no meeting. Sent 24 hours ahead, with the decision to be made named.

03

25 or 50 minutes. Default lengths, so back-to-back days always have a gap.

04

Notes within 24 hours. Decisions and owners posted in the team channel.

Do this

· Post the decision in writing, even after a hallway chat
· Name a remote co-host to watch chat and raised hands
· Book cross-team workshops on Tuesday or Wednesday

Not this

· Deciding in a room and telling remote colleagues later
· A shared laptop on a conference table as the only camera
· "Quick sync?" pings during someone's protected focus block
