Hybrid Work Policy · v1.0

04 — Policy

One goal, three commitments

Everything in this policy exists to serve a single purpose statement, delivered through three commitments and one clear boundary.

Policy purpose

Make time together deliberate, and time apart productive — with the same rules for every comparable role.

01 · CONNECTION

Two anchor days, company-wide

Tuesday and Wednesday are shared, so collaboration is scheduled.

02 · FOCUS

Two remote days, genuinely protected

No status meetings, no all-hands, no early recurring invites.

03 · FAIRNESS

One standard, documented exceptions

Exceptions are written, time-bound and reviewed quarterly.

In scope

All 4,200 salaried employees, 11 countries, grade 3 and above.

Out of scope

Site-essential roles and contractors on client-site terms.
