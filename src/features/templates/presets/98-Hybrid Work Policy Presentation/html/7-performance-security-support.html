Hybrid Work Policy · v1.0

07 — Safeguards

Three safeguards behind the flexibility

Flexibility only holds if performance stays outcome-based, data stays protected, and people stay supported.

Performance

Judged on outcomes, never on presence

· Quarterly goals, reviewed every six weeks
· Location never affects ratings, pay or promotion
· One documented 1:1 per fortnight
· Calibration uses delivery evidence only

Manager commitment

All people managers complete the Leading Hybrid Teams module before 1 March.

Security

Same protection, wherever you open the laptop

· Company device only for client data
· VPN plus passkey MFA on every system
· No confidential calls in shared spaces
· Lost device reported within 4 hours

Non-negotiable

Working abroad needs written approval — tax and data rules differ by country.

Support

Practical help, not just permission

· £350 home-desk allowance via Expenses
· Ergonomic assessment on request
· 24/7 confidential counselling line
· Right to disconnect after hours

New joiner support

Three on-site days a week in month one, with a named buddy.
