09 — FAQs

Questions we hear most

Straight answers from the pilot Q&A sessions — the full list of 24 questions sits on the policy hub.

Can I swap an anchor day for a different one?

Only for a specific reason and with your lead's agreement in writing — for example a customer visit. Anchor days are not a personal allocation to move around each week.

What if my team is spread across time zones?

Core hours are local, so overlap is agreed at team level. Distributed teams publish a written overlap window and rotate meeting times so the cost is shared fairly.

Does working from home affect my rating or promotion?

No. Ratings use agreed goals and delivery evidence only. Badge and booking data are never shared with managers or used in calibration.

I have caring duties or an accessibility need — what then?

Raise a formal flexible-working request in Workday. Your People Partner responds within 10 working days, and approved adjustments sit outside the standard pattern.

Who decides my team day, and can it change?

Your lead sets it and publishes it a month ahead. It can be reset once per quarter, so the calendar you plan around stays stable.

What happens if the pattern is repeatedly ignored?

A supportive conversation first, then a documented plan with your lead and People Partner. Formal process only if the pattern continues without an agreed reason.

Still unsure? Ask in the #hybrid-work channel, or book a 15-minute slot with your People Partner.
