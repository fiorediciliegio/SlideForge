TOKYO · NEIGHBOURHOOD ADVENTURES
Three Tokyos, Twenty
Minutes Apart
Pick one district per half-day. The city changes character faster than it changes distance — each of these is a short hop from the last.

OLD-TOWN LANES

Yanaka

Low wooden houses, cats on walls, and a slope of small shops that has escaped redevelopment. Sound drops the moment you leave the main road.

Go for · unhurried walking

Best light: late afternoon. Allow 2–3 hours on foot.

SECONDHAND & STAGE

Shimokitazawa

A maze of narrow pedestrian streets stacked with used-clothing racks, tiny record rooms, small theatres and live music floors above street level.

Go for · browsing & night energy

Livens up after dark. Cash is handy in the smallest shops.

RIVERSIDE WORKSHOPS

Kuramae

Former warehouses turned into maker studios — leather, coffee roasting, notebooks bound by hand — strung along a slow, wide river.

Go for · makers & river air

Small studios keep irregular days — [check before travelling].

Plan it as a chain, not a list: old-town slope in the afternoon, secondhand streets at dusk, riverside studios the next morning.
