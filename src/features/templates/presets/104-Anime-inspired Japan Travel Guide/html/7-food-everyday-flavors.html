FOOD & EVERYDAY FLAVOURS
Eat at the
Counter, Not
the Landmark
Six seats, one cook, a ticket machine at the door. Small specialist places do one dish properly — and welcome solo diners without ceremony.

Table manners, briefly

Say thanks before and after eating. Slurping noodles is fine. Don't stick chopsticks upright in rice, and don't tip — it isn't expected.

Ordering without the language

Many counters use a ticket machine with photo buttons. Point, pay, hand the ticket over. Picture menus are common.

FOUR FLAVOURS TO FIND

Noodle counters

Hot broth bowls and cold dipping styles, eaten fast.

Corner shops

Rice triangles, egg sandwiches, cold tea.

Market lanes

Grilled skewers and pickles, eaten standing up.
