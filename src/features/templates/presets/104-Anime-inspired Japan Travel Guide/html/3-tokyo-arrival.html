TOKYO · FIRST ARRIVAL
Tokyo Begins
at the Ticket Gate
Your first hour is the city's best introduction. Read it slowly instead of racing to the hotel.

Tap, don't queue

One rechargeable travel card opens the gates and pays at most shop counters. Top it up at the machines beside them.

Trust the exit number

Big stations have dozens of exits. Note the number, not the street name — it saves a long underground detour.

First stop: the corner shop

Cold drinks, hot food, cash machines and a clean counter — often open late or around the clock.

Crossings set the tempo

Signals chime, taxis glide, machines glow at every corner. Nobody hurries the light.
