Buy the Thing Made
Two Streets Away
Airport gift aisles sell the same box to everyone. These five categories send you home with something a neighbourhood actually produces.

Stationery

Fine-tip pens, notebooks with paper worth writing on, masking tape by the metre.

Books & prints

Secondhand art books, photo zines and woodblock reprints from small dealers.

Ceramics

Rice bowls and small cups from studio shelves. Ask for padding before packing.

Secondhand style

Sorted racks, honest condition labels, workwear and jackets by the kilo.

Character goods

Capsule machines and hobby floors — cheap, specific and easy to carry home.

How to tell a real find from a gift-shop filler

Look for a maker's name, a workshop behind the counter, or a shopkeeper who can explain how it was made. If the identical item is stacked in every station kiosk, it travelled further than you did.
