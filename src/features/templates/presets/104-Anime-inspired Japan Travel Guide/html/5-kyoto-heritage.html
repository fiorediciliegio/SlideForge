the Slow Visitor
Shrines and temples are working places of worship, not attractions on a checklist. Arrive early, move quietly, and read the signs at each entrance — rules differ site to site.

Stone-paved approaches

Lantern rows, moss and raked gravel. Walk the edges rather than straight up the middle.

Water basin first

Rinse hands before approaching the hall. Etiquette notices are posted on site.
