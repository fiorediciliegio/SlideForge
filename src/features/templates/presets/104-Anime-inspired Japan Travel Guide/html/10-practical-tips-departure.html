PRACTICAL TIPS · DEPARTURE
Sort These Six Things
Before You Fly

Transport

Travel card for local trips; a rail pass only for long legs.

Connectivity

Data plan or pocket router, with offline maps saved early.

Payment

Cards work widely; keep cash for small shops and shrines.

Weather

Layers plus a folding umbrella; check the forecast nightly.

Packing

Broken-in shoes, a foldable bag, slip-on temple footwear.

Etiquette

Calls off trains, rubbish carried, voices low in lanes.

VERIFY BEFORE DEPARTURE

Entry rules, rail pass eligibility, tax-free limits and site opening days all change. [Confirm with official sources]

You will forget the timetable and remember the platform breeze, the corner shop at midnight, and the lane you found by turning the wrong way.
