Pacing rules that keep the trip enjoyable

Two anchors a day maximum. Ninety unplanned minutes after lunch. Never schedule a sight straight after a long train.

Photograph people with permission

Ask before close portraits, skip private doorways and residential windows, and respect every posted no-camera sign.
