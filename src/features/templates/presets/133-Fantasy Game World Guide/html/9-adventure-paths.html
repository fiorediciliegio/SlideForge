Neyra Vash's working sheet — creases, thumb-marks, and all three departures from the Brass Gate
I · The Cinnabar Climb
Hadaresh → the escarpment galleries · 14 days
The League will not sell passage up the rope bridges to anyone carrying a throne writ, so the papers must be hidden, forged, or renounced at the first gatehouse.
The choice — climb as nobody, or arrive as a guest of the guild-halls and be watched the whole way.
II · The Salt Walk
Hadaresh → the eastern vents · 9 nights
Walked between dusk and the fourth hour. The staked line was re-cut this year and the old stakes still stand, so half the markers now lead onto thin crust.
The choice — trust the guide's count of stakes, or trust the crawlers that gather where the crust is weakest.
III · The Tide Run
Hadaresh → the delta anchorages · 11 days
The fastest road, and the only one that ends outside imperial law. Passage is bought with a knot tied into a stranger's net — a debt no writ can discharge.
The choice — be adopted by a tide-council and inherit its quarrels, or pay in coin and travel as cargo.
Rising stake: each route ends holding something the weir at Sabb needs.
