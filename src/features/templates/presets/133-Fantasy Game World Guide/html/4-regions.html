Terraced, irrigated,
and rationed
Hot ochre floodplain under aqueduct arches; the only ground in Sarkhet that yields two grain harvests. Brass-domed granaries are guarded like armouries.
Yields — barley, date sugar, fired brick
Danger — heat collapse, ration riots
Worth — feeds the whole empire
A dead sea walked
only at night
Cracked white crust over brine, sixty leagues wide. Ships from the old shoreline still stand upright where the water left them, timbers preserved by salt.
Yields — pan salt, natron, glass sand
Danger — crust breaks, no shade at noon
Worth — the shortest road east
Red cliffs cut into
cell and gallery
A thousand-foot cinnabar wall running north to south. Dwellings, foundries and rope bridges are carved directly into the rock face above the mine galleries.
Yields — cinnabar, copper, firebrick clay
Danger — quicksilver sickness, rockfall
Worth — every kiln's lining
Green, wet, and
barely governed
Monsoon mangrove delta on stilts. Four months of rain a year make it the wettest ground on the continent and the hardest to march an army across.
Yields — pepper, hardwood, dyed cloth
Danger — fever, tide-flood, piracy
Worth — Sarkhet's only deep harbour
