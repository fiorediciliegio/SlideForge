The Foundational Age
The Brass Concord, Year 0
Nine brickmasters agreed a single law: fire would be held in kilns, never in persons. Nine sun-kilns were raised along the volcanic north-east, and the empire that grew beneath them measured its years from that kindling. Every road, tax, and marriage since has been arranged around fuel.
The Tension of the Present Year
Two Failures at Once, 812 SR
The River Ossir has dropped one hand-span every year for eleven years, and the throne that rations it is held by a woman of eighty-one with no named heir. Fuel can be hauled. Succession can be argued. Water can be neither, and every party in Sarkhet now knows it.

The inland sea retreats sixty leagues in one lifetime. A sea-power becomes a salt-power.

812 SR · This Year
The Ossir falls, the throne stands empty of heirs, and four powers begin to move.
