Four Powers, No Majority
No faction can hold Sarkhet alone, and every possible pair of allies is already quarrelling over something older than the drought.

The Kiln Throne
Seat: Hadaresh · 4,000 sworn
Holds the imperial water writ and the eight lit kilns. Its aim is continuity: keep the fires and the ration lawful one more year.
Sign — kiln mouth under a cracked crown
The Salt Covenant
Seat: Sabb · assembly of bowls
A judicial brotherhood grown from the Bowl-Sworn. It seeks to void the imperial writ and place the Ossir under sworn arbitration.
Sign — bowl above cracked crust, roped
The Cinnabar League
Seat: Vel Cinnabar · 31 guild-halls
Mine-owners who line every kiln in Sarkhet. They want the brick levy repaid in water rights, and are pricing the throne's survival.
Sign — crossed picks on a stepped cliff
The Free Ports
Seat: Mirrowgate · 9 tide-councils
Net-Kin councils that have withheld the monsoon tithe for three years. They intend secession before the drought reaches the delta.
Sign — mangrove and crossed oars
The Flashpoint of 812
The Weir at Sabb
Whoever holds the Sabb weir decides how much of the Ossir reaches the basin. The throne garrisons it, the Covenant has occupied its steps for forty days, the League has offered to buy it outright, and the Ports have stopped paying.
One gate. Four claims. No arbiter all four accept.
