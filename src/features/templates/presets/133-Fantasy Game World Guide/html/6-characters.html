Chapter VI · Persons of Consequence
Five Hands Reaching for the Same Water
Sethra the Eighty-First
Kiln Throne · aged 81
Rations the Ossir by her own hand and refuses to name an heir, believing any named successor will be dead within a season. Wants the river to outlive her.
Neyra Vash
Salt Walk guide · Ledger Houses
Has crossed the Pale Sea two hundred times and lost only nine travellers. Sells the crossing to anyone; her price is a debt she chooses later.
Warden Orbec Hail
Furnace-knight · Kiln Throne
Sworn to keep the eight kilns lit, and increasingly certain the throne is the wrong instrument for it. Would crown a competent stranger over a lawful fool.
The Blind Assessor
Bowl-Sworn · name surrendered
Arbitrates water claims no court will touch, and has begun ruling against the throne itself. Believes the Ossir is being taken, not merely failing.
Little Tam Ossiri
Mirrowgate runner · Net-Kin
Nineteen, and the only person alive who has swum the sealed cistern under Old Vaunt. Carries a surname the throne retired eighty years ago.
