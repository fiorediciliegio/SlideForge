03 · Segments & Needs
Five segments, unequal weight —
the need behind each is different
Bubble area = share of 3.1M active base · colour = revenue index (base = 100) · illustrative
Zoom · two priority personas
Habitual Creators
14% of base · 24% of revenue
Need: speed and control — publish in under two minutes without losing formatting.
Friction: export limits force a detour to desktop tools.
Says: “I'll pay for the version that stops slowing me down.”
Value Seekers
21% of base · 23% of revenue
Need: proof the paid tier is worth it before committing a full year.
Friction: annual-only pricing reads as risk, not saving.
Says: “Let me try it monthly and I'll decide by the second bill.”
Shares and revenue indices are illustrative assumptions for review.
