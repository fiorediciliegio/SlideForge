CONTROL GATES
G1 Model governance
No scorecard reaches production without documented development, validation, and an approved owner.
G2 Fair-lending testing
Disparity metrics computed on every policy version before release, and monthly in production.
G3 Segregation of duties
Policy author, approver, and deployer are enforced as three distinct roles.
G4 Drift & override monitoring
Population shift and manual-override rates alert the risk console daily.
G5 Examiner export
Any application reproducible end-to-end, including the policy version in force that day.
