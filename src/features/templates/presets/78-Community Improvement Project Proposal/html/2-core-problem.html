02 — The Problem

Maple Ward grew by 18%.
Its shared space did not.

Since 2018 the ward has added 1,340 residents and no new public space. The one candidate site — a 1.4-acre lot at Maple & 8th — has sat fenced and vacant for eleven years.

“My kids play in the lane behind the laundromat because there is nowhere else. Everyone here knows it. Nobody has fixed it.”

Denise O., resident 14 years · Maple Street
