05 — Goals & Design Principles

Four principles. Each one decides something concrete on the site.

Principles were ratified by open vote at the 14 July resident assembly — 96 of 118 attendees in favour.

Principle

What it commits us to

Design decision on site

01 Open to everyone,
every day

No gate fees, no membership, no booking needed for the open ground.

Step-free entry on both streets · lighting to 8pm · signage in 4 languages

02 Residents hold
the decisions

A resident majority on the stewardship board, with budget authority.

7 of 11 board seats elected by ward residents · quarterly open books

03 Cheap to run,
built to last

Operating cost must be coverable without a new grant by year three.

Rainwater irrigation · hard-wearing surfaces · $47k/yr steady-state budget

04 Visible progress
within a year

Something usable opens before the full build finishes.

Phase 1 grove and plaza open month 9, ahead of the pavilion
