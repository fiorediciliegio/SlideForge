Concept site plan · 1.4 acres · Maple & 8th

What goes in each zone

1 Shade grove. 34 semi-mature native trees, permeable paths, 18 benches.
2 Play & court. Under-12 play structure, half-court, sightlines from all sides.
3 Pavilion. 90 m² all-weather room, washrooms, kitchenette, tool library.
4 Growing plots. 40 raised beds, 12 reserved for the food bank harvest.
5 Entry plaza. Step-free entrance, noticeboard, market stalls on Saturdays.
