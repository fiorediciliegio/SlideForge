07 — Participation & Governance

Every resident has a route in — from one hour to a board seat.

The ladder is deliberate: people who show up once become the people who run the site.

Ladder of participation · year-one targets

LEAD Stewardship board seat Sets budget, hires the coordinator, approves programs
11 people
RUN Program lead or crew chief Owns one recurring program end to end
18 people
COMMIT Trained rota volunteer Two shifts a month, safeguarding trained
40 people
SHOW UP Drop-in user or build-day helper No sign-up, no commitment, no cost
1,200 people

Who sits on the board

7 Elected ward residents
2 Youth delegates (13–19)
1 City parks liaison
1 Partner org representative

Accountability

Open books published quarterly. Annual resident assembly can recall any board member by simple majority.
