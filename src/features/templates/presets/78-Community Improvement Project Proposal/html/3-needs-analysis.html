03 — Needs Analysis

We knocked on 412 doors. Shade, safety and somewhere to sit came first.

Method

Door-to-door survey (412 households, 71% response), 6 kitchen-table sessions, 3 school workshops. Apr–Jun 2026.

Top requests · share of households naming this a priority
Shade & trees 78%
Safe play space for under-12s 71%
Seating & lighting after dark 64%
Indoor room for meetings & classes 52%
Growing plots & food access 39%
Who we heard from
34% Families with children under 12
27% Residents aged 60+, many living alone
22% Newcomer households, 9 languages
17% Youth 13–19, via Maple High

Four in five respondents said they would use the site weekly if it were shaded and staffed.
