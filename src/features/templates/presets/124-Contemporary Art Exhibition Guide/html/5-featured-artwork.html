Featured Work
Cobalt
Studies (V)
Elin Sørensen, 2024
Medium Egg tempera and pigment on birch panel
Dimensions 220 × 165 cm
Provenance Courtesy the artist & Galleri Riis, Oslo
Room Hall C · Room 2 · North Wall
Interpretation
Sørensen builds each panel from thirty-plus layers of thinned tempera. Seen from a distance the field appears solid; on approach the surface reveals a slow tremor of hand, a residue of decisions.
The Cobalt Studies refuse the sublime. Their scale invites the body without swallowing it.
