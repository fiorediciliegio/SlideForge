Entering Mid-Cycle
No work has a queue.
Every timed piece loops without an announced start. Walking in halfway is the intended condition, not a compromise.
Hall B is darkened.
Allow ninety seconds for your eyes to adjust before judging the image. Seating is available along the rear wall.
One work happens once.
Aylor's performance is live and unrecorded. It occurs Saturdays at 15:00 in Hall D and is not repeated on other days.
Reading  —  Seen end to end, the timed works occupy roughly as long as the suggested walk through all four halls. The exhibition therefore cannot be completed in one visit at full duration; the schedule is designed to be sampled, not exhausted.
