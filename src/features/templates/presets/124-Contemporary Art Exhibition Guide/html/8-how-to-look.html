Fig. 03 — Sightlines in a densely hung room
Common habit
Practice we suggest instead
Reading the wall label first Look for sixty seconds, then read. The caption is a second opinion, not an instruction.
Photographing and moving on Stand at two distances — three metres, then thirty centimetres. Most works change completely between them.
Trying to see everything Choose four works and stay with them. A partial visit held in memory beats a complete one that isn't.
Moving when the room moves Let the group pass. Every room in this exhibition reads differently once it empties.
Twenty minutes in one hall is a complete visit. So is two hours in four.
