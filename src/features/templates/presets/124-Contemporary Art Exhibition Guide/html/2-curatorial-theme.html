The Proposition
Listening
as a form of
looking.
Quiet Frequencies gathers twelve artists whose practices resist the noise of the image economy. Across painting, sculpture, moving image and sound, the exhibition proposes attention itself as a material — slow, patient, and unresolved.
The works are arranged not by medium but by tempo: rooms of near-silence give way to more insistent registers, then return.
Three Movements
I.
Ground
Surfaces, sediments, weight. Works that hold the room.
II.
Interval
Pauses, negative space, the pause before recognition.
III.
Signal
Message, transmission, the return of the voice.
