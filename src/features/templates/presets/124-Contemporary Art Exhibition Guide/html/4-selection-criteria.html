Why These Twelve
The selection was not made by medium or by generation. Each work was tested against how long it takes to become legible, and how much physical matter it puts in the room.

Three Tests Applied to Every Work
01  Does it withstand a second visit?
Works that resolve completely on first contact were set aside. The twelve selected change on return.
02  Does it need the room?
Each work depends on scale, acoustics or sightline. None would survive intact as a reproduction.
03  Does it hold without explanation?
Wall texts are deliberately short. A work that collapses without its caption was not included.
