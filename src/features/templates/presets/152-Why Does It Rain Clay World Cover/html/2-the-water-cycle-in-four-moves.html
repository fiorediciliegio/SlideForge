The Water Cyclein Four Moves

Water keeps moving between the ground, the air,
and the clouds — again and again.
01

EVAPORATION

Sunlight warms water. Some of it changes into invisible water vapor and rises.

02

CONDENSATION

Higher up, the air is cooler. Water vapor gathers into tiny droplets that form clouds.

03

PRECIPITATION

When cloud droplets become heavy enough, they fall as rain.

04

COLLECTION

Water gathers in puddles, rivers, lakes, and oceans — ready to begin again.
