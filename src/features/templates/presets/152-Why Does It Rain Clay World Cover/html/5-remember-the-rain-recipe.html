Remember the Rain Recipe

Four simple ideas explain the whole journey.
01

HEAT

Sunlight warms water and helps it enter the air as vapor.

02

COOL

Rising vapor meets cooler air and forms tiny droplets.

03

GROW

Droplets join together until the cloud can no longer hold them.

04

RETURN

Rain falls, gathers, and starts the cycle all over again.

RAIN = WATER + HEAT + COOLING + TIME
The water cycle keeps Earth’s water moving.
