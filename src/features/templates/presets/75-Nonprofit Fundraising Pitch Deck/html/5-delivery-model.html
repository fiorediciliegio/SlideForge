04 · Delivery model
From donated pallet to a child's kitchen in 48 hours
A five-step operating chain, run by 6 staff and 140 trained volunteers.
Step 01 · Wed Source Grocery rescue, the regional food bank, and 3 farms deliver to the hub.
Step 02 · Thu Pack Volunteer shifts assemble 1,050 kits against a fixed nutrition spec.
Step 03 · Fri Route Two vans cover 61 stops on a published schedule, 2–6pm.
Step 04 · Fri Hand off A site host greets by name, offers help, and logs the pickup.
Step 05 · Mon Learn Site counts and teacher check-ins shape next week's order.
Trusted sites, not new buildings Schools, parishes and clinics host at no cost — zero real-estate overhead. One shared intake record Consent-based tracking lets partners follow up without re-interviewing families. Weekly cost discipline Route cost per child is reviewed every Monday against a $9.40 target.
