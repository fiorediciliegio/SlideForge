01 · The problem
School lunch ends on Friday. Hunger does not.
1 in 4 children in Kern Valley live in a household that runs out of food before the month ends.
Federal meal programs cover 10 of 21 weekly meals — and stop at 3pm Friday. The nearest full-service grocery is 14 miles from the two highest-need districts. Teachers report Monday morning as the week's worst hour for focus.
