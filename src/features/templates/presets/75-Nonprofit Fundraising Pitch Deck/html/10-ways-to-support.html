09 · Ways to support
Choose the level that fits — we report back on it
Every gift is expressed in children served, not in overhead lines.
Sustainer $50/ mo Feeds one child every weekend of the school year. Quarterly impact note from a host site.
Site sponsor $15K Opens and staffs one new neighborhood pickup site for a year. Named site, annual visit invitation.
Route underwriter $120K Funds the third van and its full Mesa Flats route. 780 additional children reached weekly.
Lead partner $500K Anchors the three-year plan and unlocks our 1:1 match. Board seat option, co-branded evaluation.
Support beyond a check Host a site — four hours each Friday. Send a packing team — 350 kits per shift. Open a door — one grocer, farm, or foundation.
Our ask today A $250,000 lead commitment before the school year begins in August. Two new routes, 18 sites, and 1,400 children who stop losing their weekends.
