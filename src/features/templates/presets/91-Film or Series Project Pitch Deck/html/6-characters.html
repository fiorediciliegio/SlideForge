The Salt Line
05 · Character Cards
Four people, one shared lie
Casting is being built around one physical lead
and three regional stage names.
Character frame · lead
Nuala Breen
46 · Salvage diver
Left at nineteen, came back for the money. Reads a wreck faster than she reads a room.
Wants: the truth. Needs: to be let back in.
Character frame · antagonist
Fintan Roe
61 · Harbour master
Kept the port alive for thirty years by deciding which losses got written down.
Wants: order. Needs: to be forgiven.
Character frame · ally
Sgt. Aoife Daly
34 · Garda sergeant
Posted in from Galway. Enforcing a law the town treats as a weather warning.
Wants: a conviction. Needs: a witness.
Character frame · wildcard
Cillian Breen
23 · Deckhand · nephew
Raised on the version of his father's death that Nuala has come home to break.
Wants: a berth. Needs: the real story.
