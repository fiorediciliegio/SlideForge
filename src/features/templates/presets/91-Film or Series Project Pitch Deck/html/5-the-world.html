A harbour that keeps its books underwater
Ardglas is a working port of 4,000 people, three trawler families and one salvage yard. Everyone is owed something. Nobody files it on paper. When the tide drops far enough, the ledger shows.

The rules of this world
Economy
Quota is currency. One licence feeds nine households, so nobody reports the ninth.
Law
One sergeant, forty miles of coast. Disputes are settled in the yard, not the court.
Faith & memory
The drowned are named on a wall. Adding a name means admitting a cause.
Danger
Every descent has a clock. Air, light and tide close the window at once.
The engine of the season
Every episode, Nuala goes down for something the town wants raised, and comes back up with something it wanted sunk.
