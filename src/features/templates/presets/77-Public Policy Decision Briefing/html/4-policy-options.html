03 · Policy options
Four interventions, two levers
Options A and B act on price; C acts on supply. Option D combines all three at reduced intensity.
Demand-side levers
A
Expand the childcare voucher
Raise the eligibility ceiling from 200% to 300% FPL and lift reimbursement to the 75th percentile of market rate.
Reaches 4,100 families in 9 months · adds no new slots · price-inflation risk in desert ZIPs
B
Refundable tax credit
A county credit of up to $3,600 per child, claimed annually through the existing tax filing channel.
Lowest admin cost at 3% · relief arrives up to 14 months late · no help with access
Supply-side lever
C
Capacity & workforce grants
Facility start-up grants in desert ZIP codes plus a $3.20/hour educator wage supplement tied to retention.
Adds 2,600 slots by year three · slowest to show results · needs provider capability
Blended
D
Blended, phased package
Targeted voucher expansion in year one, capacity and wage grants from year two, credit deferred pending review.
Reaches 5,900 families and adds 2,100 slots · highest coordination load
