R1
Provider rate inflation absorbs the subsidy
Cap reimbursement at the 75th percentile and publish quarterly rate monitoring. Owner: Budget Office
R2
Educator pipeline cannot staff new rooms
Fund 220 apprenticeship places with the community college before grants open. Owner: Workforce Board
R3
Federal CCDF match is reduced mid-cycle
Hold a 9% contingency and stage year-two grants on confirmed award letters. Owner: Finance
R4
Licensing throughput delays site openings
Add three inspectors and a 45-day service standard for grant-funded sites. Owner: Licensing
R5
Low take-up among eligible rural households
Route applications through school enrolment and library outreach in desert ZIPs. Owner: Community Services
