04 · Delivery mechanism
From application to placement in 21 days
One intake, two funding streams. Families and providers enter through the same case system, so eligibility and capacity are matched in a single queue.
Family pathway — demand side
Day 0–1
Single online intake
One form covering voucher, sliding-scale fee and priority-placement flags.
Day 2–7
Automated eligibility
Income verified against state wage records; 71% cleared without a caseworker.
Day 8–14
Capacity matching
Live provider vacancy feed offers up to three placements within 8km.
Day 15–21
Placement & payment
Subsidy paid directly to the provider; family pays the capped co-share only.
Provider pathway — supply side
Capacity grant
Up to $180k per site for build-out and licensing in desert ZIP codes.
Wage supplement
$3.20/hour per credentialed educator, paid quarterly on retention evidence.
Quality & compliance
Annual licensing audit plus unannounced ratio checks twice per year.
Shared data layer
Vacancy, subsidy and workforce records in one monthly reporting cycle.
