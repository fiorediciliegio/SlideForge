09 · Recommendation & next steps
Approve Option D and the FY2027 appropriation
Decision requested
Adopt the blended, phased package — Option D
Appropriate $71M for FY2027, delegate rate-setting authority to the Office of Policy & Budget, and require a public progress report each quarter.
Why Option D
—Highest weighted score at 3.90, and never the weakest on any criterion.
—Acts on price and supply together, so relief does not simply raise market rates.
—Phasing keeps FY2027 exposure at $71M with a review gate before scale-up.
