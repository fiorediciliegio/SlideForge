Co-pay is capped at 10% of household income and never exceeds $210 per month per child. Families above 300% FPL retain access at full market rate.
Priority placement rules
—Infant and toddler placements are reserved at 45% of all new capacity created under the programme.
—Households in the 12 desert ZIP codes receive first offer on any newly licensed site within their district.
—Non-standard-hour demand carries a 1.15× reimbursement multiplier to make evening care viable.
—Children with documented disabilities receive an inclusion supplement of up to $4,100 per year.
