Study design & key data · Study GX441-PK07
30 mg/kg preserved 82% of nerve fibre density through the full paclitaxel course
Primary endpoint, day 56
+3.5 fibres/mm vs vehicle
95% CI 2.4–4.6 · p = 0.002
Sample
Male Sprague-Dawley rats, 4 arms × n = 12, randomised by baseline IENF; 2 animals excluded (dosing error).
Method
PGP9.5 immunostain of footpad biopsy, blinded manual count; 3 sections per animal per timepoint.
Statistics
Baseline-adjusted ANCOVA, Dunnett correction vs vehicle; error bars = SEM; α = 0.05, two-sided.
Caveat
Single species, single schedule. Tumour-model co-dosing not yet run — see risk R4 on the next slide.
