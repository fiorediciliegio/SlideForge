01 · No machine signal
68% of stoppages are logged by hand, on average 42 minutes after the event — and 24 of the 62 machines emit no data at all.
02 · Disconnected systems
ERP, a spreadsheet MES and paper travelers never share a part genealogy — the same order is keyed in three times.
03 · Reactive maintenance
82% of work orders are breakdown-driven, and there is no condition data on the 44 assets behind most stoppage hours.
04 · Decisions at handover
Supervisors see yesterday's numbers at 09:00. By then the shift that lost the hours has already gone home.
The constraint is not capacity — Plant 2 already has it. The constraint is that nobody sees the loss while it is still happening.
