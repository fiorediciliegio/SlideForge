Three · History & Character
Three building periods still share the same street plan.
I The walled core [Era · dates]
Narrow, irregular lanes that ignore the grid, a market square where roads converge, and house plots one cart-width wide.
II The merchant century [Era · dates]
Façades grow taller than the houses behind them: carved gables, guild emblems, wide loading doors turned toward the water.
III Boulevards & infill [Era · dates]
Ramparts become ring roads, tram tracks cut through, and plainer twentieth-century blocks fill the gaps.
Same plan, three handwritings — all readable in one street.

The character of the place
Stone underfoot everywhere, so the city sounds different from a modern one: heels, bicycle bells, shutters.

Colour is limited and warm — render in ochre and dusty pink, roofs in terracotta, ironwork in dark green.

Life runs on fixed hours rather than late nights: early markets, long lunches, quiet afternoons.
