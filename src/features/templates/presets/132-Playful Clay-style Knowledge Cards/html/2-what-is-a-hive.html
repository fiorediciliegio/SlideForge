Card 02  ·  What it is

A hive is one animal made of many bees

A honeybee colony is called a superorganism: thousands of bees behaving like the cells of a single body. Every bee is born into one of three jobs.

The question these cards answer

If no bee is in charge, how does a hive stay warm, fed and organised?
