Card 08  ·  Myth vs fact

Three things almost everyone gets wrong

On the left, what people say. On the right, what scientists actually find.

Myth

“Every bee makes honey in a hive.”

Myth

“The queen gives the orders.”

Myth

“Any bee that stings you dies.”

Fact

Around 20,000 bee species exist, and over 90% are solitary.

Most nest alone in soil or hollow stems. Honey-making hives are the rare exception.

Fact

She lays eggs; she does not command.

Big choices — a new nest site, or replacing the queen — are made collectively by the workers.

Fact

Only honeybee workers do. Drones have no sting at all.

A worker’s sting is barbed, so it tears free. Bumblebees and wasps have smooth stings and survive.
