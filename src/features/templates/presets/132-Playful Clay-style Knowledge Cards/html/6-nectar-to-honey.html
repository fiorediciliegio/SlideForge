Card 06  ·  How it works

Honey is nectar with the water taken out

Follow one drop along the clay path. Nectar arrives about 70% water and leaves as honey at about 17%.

70%

Nectar water

17%

Honey water

1

Sip

A forager drinks nectar through her straw-like tongue.

2

Store & mix

It rides in her honey stomach, where enzymes start working.

3

Hand over

Back home she passes the drop mouth-to-mouth to a house bee.

4

Fan it dry

Spread thin in a cell, then fanned by beating wings until thick.

5

Cap it

A wax lid seals the cell, and the honey keeps for years.
