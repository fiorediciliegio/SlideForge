Card 04  ·  Three core facts

Three numbers: her time, her home, her workload

Each fact measures a different part of a worker bee’s world.

6 weeks

Her summer lifespan

A worker born in summer lives about six weeks. A bee born in autumn survives the whole winter — up to six months.

35 °C

The temperature of the nursery

Bees hold the brood nest near 35 °C (95 °F) all year by shivering to warm it and fanning to cool it.

2 million

Flower visits per jar

One 500 g (1 lb) jar of honey takes about two million flower visits by the whole colony.
