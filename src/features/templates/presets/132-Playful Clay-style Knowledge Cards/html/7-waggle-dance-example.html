Card 07  ·  Example in action

One bee dances the map, and the hive reads it

A returning forager runs a figure-eight on the comb. The straight middle part — the waggle run — carries two pieces of information at once.

Home hive

Flower patch

Foragers usually work 2–3 km (1.5–2 mi) from home, and further when food is scarce.

Angle = direction

Straight up on the comb means “fly toward the sun.” Tilt the run 40° and the food sits 40° from the sun.

Length = distance

A longer waggle means a longer flight. A 2.5-second run points to food about 2,625 m away.
