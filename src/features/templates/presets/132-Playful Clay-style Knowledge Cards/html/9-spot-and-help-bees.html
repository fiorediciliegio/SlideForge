Card 09  ·  Try it outside

Next sunny day, check the legs before you judge

Three insects visit the same flowers. These clues tell them apart from a safe distance.

Honeybee

Slim amber body, lightly furry, and orange pollen packs on the back legs.

Look for: leg baskets

Bumblebee

Big, round and fuzzy all over, with a deep buzz and a slower flight.

Look for: thick fur

Wasp

Smooth and shiny with a pinched waist and no pollen on its legs.

Look for: narrow waist

Be a good neighbour

1

Stay still

Never swat. A foraging bee is not hunting you.

2

Set a table

Native flowers, plus a pebble dish of water.

Your field mission: watch one flower for two minutes and count how many visitors carry pollen.
