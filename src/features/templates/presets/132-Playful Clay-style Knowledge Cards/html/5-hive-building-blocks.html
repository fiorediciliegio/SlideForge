Card 05  ·  Building blocks

A hive is stacked in floors

Pull a hive apart and it is one material — beeswax — shaped into three zones. Food goes on top, babies in the middle, the front door below.

New word: beeswax

Workers squeeze wax from eight glands under their belly. Making 1 kg of wax costs about 8 kg of honey.

Why hexagons?

They tile with no gaps and use the least wax per cell.

Honey store

Capped cells at the top — the winter pantry

Pollen store

Packed colourful pollen, the protein food

Brood nest

Where eggs and larvae grow, kept at 35 °C

Entrance

One guarded doorway for the whole colony
