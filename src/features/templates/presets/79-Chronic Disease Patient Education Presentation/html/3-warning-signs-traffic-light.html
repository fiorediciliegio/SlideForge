02 · Signs, risks and when to act
Green, amber, red
You do not need to memorise a symptom list — just know which of these three rows you are standing in today.
GREEN
On track — keep going
Balanced plates, movement every day, medicine taken, feet checked. Readings mostly 80–130 mg/dL (4.4–7.2 mmol/L) before meals and under 180 mg/dL (10.0 mmol/L) after.
Nothing to fix. Bring your log to your next visit.
AMBER
Call your team within days
More thirst or urination · blurred vision · tingling hands or feet · sores healing slowly · unexplained weight loss · readings above your target several days running.
Phone your clinic. Don't wait for the next appointment.
RED
Emergency — get help now
Confusion, seizure or cannot be woken (a severe low, under 54 mg/dL / 3.0 mmol/L) · glucose 300 mg/dL (16.7 mmol/L) or above with vomiting, fruity breath or fast breathing · chest pain · sudden one-sided weakness or slurred speech.
Call 911 or go to the ER. Do not drive yourself.
Sources: ADA Standards of Care 2025, §6 Tables 6.3 & 6.4 · CDC, Diabetic Ketoacidosis & Low Blood Sugar (2024) · NIDDK, Diabetes, Heart Disease & Stroke
