01 · Understanding the condition
What type 2 diabetes really is
It is a problem with a key, not a punishment for eating sugar.
Body diagram
Simplified flat torso: pancreas releasing insulin “keys”, glucose dots in the bloodstream, a cell door that will not open.
1
Food becomes glucose — sugar travelling in your blood.
2
Insulin is the key that lets glucose into your cells for energy.
3
In type 2, the key stops fitting well — so glucose stays in the blood.
1 in 9
adults worldwide live with diabetes — 589 million people. Around 90–95% have type 2. You are far from alone.
Four myths worth dropping today
MYTH “Eating sugar caused this.”
FACT Sugary drinks and extra weight raise the risk — sugar alone is not the cause.
MYTH “It's a mild condition.”
FACT Left unmanaged it roughly doubles the risk of heart disease or stroke — which is exactly why daily habits matter.
MYTH “Needing insulin means I failed.”
FACT The body makes less insulin over time. A new medicine is a normal next step, not a verdict.
MYTH “No more carbs. No more fruit.”
FACT No food is banned. Portion and balance matter far more than avoidance.
Sources: CDC, About Type 2 Diabetes (2024) · ADA Diabetes Myths · ADA Standards of Care 2025, §6 · IDF Atlas (2025) · MedlinePlus/NIH
