03 · Treatment and daily management
One ordinary day
Treatment is not an event. It is five small moments you already pass through every day.
MORNING
Wake, check, take
Same wake time. Check glucose if your plan says so. Take your medicine — even on days you feel fine.
MIDDAY
Build the plate
Use the 9-inch plate below. Then a 10-minute walk after eating — short counts.
AFTERNOON
Break up the sitting
Stand and move every 30 minutes. Long unbroken sitting works against you.
EVENING
Dinner, walk, feet
Balanced dinner, another short walk, then the two-minute foot check — tops, soles, between toes.
NIGHT
Sleep is treatment
Aim for 7 to 9 hours. Too little sleep makes glucose harder to manage the next day.
The 9-inch plate
½ non-starchy vegetables — salad, greens
¼ lean protein — fish, chicken, beans
¼ carb foods — rice, bread, fruit
About a business envelope across. Drink water.
What the week should add up to
150
minutes of moderate activity a week — brisk walking counts.
2×
strength sessions a week — bands, weights or body weight.
0
medicines stopped on your own — ask before you pause.
