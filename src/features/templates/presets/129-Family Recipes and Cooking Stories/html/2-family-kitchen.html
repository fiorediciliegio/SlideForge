the family kitchen
CHAPTER ONE
Every recipe in this book started here — at the round table by the window, where the kettle whistled and someone was always stirring something.
Grandma kept jars of tomato preserves on the shelf and a wooden spoon that has stirred more sauce than any of us can count. Sunday mornings smelled like butter and warm bread; holidays smelled like cinnamon and orange peel. These pages hold what we could write down — and remind us of everything we still cook from memory.
🍅 tomato garden 🥖 warm bread ☕ kettle on
