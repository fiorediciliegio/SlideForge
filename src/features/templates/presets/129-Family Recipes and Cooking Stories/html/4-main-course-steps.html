CHAPTER THREE  ·  MAIN COURSE
Nonna's Sunday Spaghetti
six little steps, one big pot, and the sauce that
starts every family fight (in the best way).
the Sunday pot
1
Warm the oil
Slice a whole head of garlic thin. Warm it slowly in good olive oil until it smells like Sunday.
2
Crush the tomatoes
Two big cans of San Marzano — crush by hand into the pot. Season with salt and a little sugar.
3
Simmer low
Two hours minimum, lid off. Stir when the mood strikes. The kitchen should smell like a hug.
4
Tear the basil
A whole bunch, tear (never cut) at the end. Rip in a knob of butter for a soft, glossy finish.
5
Cook the pasta
Water salty as the sea. Spaghetti al dente. Save a cup of the starchy water before draining.
6
Toss & serve!
Marry the pasta to the sauce in the pan. Splash of pasta water. Parmesan snowfall. Call everyone!
