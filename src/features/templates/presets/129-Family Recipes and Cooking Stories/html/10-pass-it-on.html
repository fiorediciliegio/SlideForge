Pass It On
three small steps to keep a recipe alive
1
Cook it together
Invite someone into the kitchen. Hands learn faster than notebooks do.
2
Write it down
Grams, cups, and the messy notes — especially the "until it smells right" parts.
3
Hand it over
Give the page away and let them change it. That's how it stays alive.
A RECIPE IS READY TO PASS ON WHEN —
✓ someone else made it and it worked
✓ the story behind it is written too
✓ the amounts are real numbers now
✓ we know whose recipe it was first
