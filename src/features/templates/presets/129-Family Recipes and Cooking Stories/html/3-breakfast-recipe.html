CHAPTER TWO  ·  BREAKFAST
Sunday
buttermilk pancakes
the recipe Dad taught us before we could reach the counter · serves 4
INGREDIENTS
· 2 cups all-purpose flour
· 2 tbsp sugar
· 1 tsp baking soda
· 1 tsp baking powder
· ½ tsp fine salt
· 2 cups buttermilk
· 2 large eggs
· 4 tbsp melted butter
· a splash of vanilla
· honey & berries to serve
METHOD
1. Whisk the dry things in a big bowl.
2. Beat eggs, buttermilk & butter together.
3. Fold wet into dry — lumps are fine!
4. Rest the batter 10 min while the pan warms.
5. ¼ cup per pancake; flip when bubbles pop.
6. Stack tall. Butter, honey, berries.
stack 'em tall
★ tip — don't overmix! lumps make the fluffiest pancakes. and warm the syrup.
