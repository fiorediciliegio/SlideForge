A photosynthesis field guide

How a Leaf Builds Sugar

Leaves use sunlight, air and water to make food. This process is called photosynthesis,
and it helps plants grow and keeps life on Earth going.
01
Sunlight
Chlorophyll in leaves captures sunlight and turns that energy into chemical energy.
Light powers the process.
02
Water + Air
Plants take in water through their roots and carbon dioxide from the air, combining them inside the leaf.
Simple ingredients. Big results.
03
Glucose + Oxygen
Using the captured energy, the leaf converts water and carbon dioxide into glucose (a sugar for food) and oxygen.
Food for plants. Oxygen for life.
