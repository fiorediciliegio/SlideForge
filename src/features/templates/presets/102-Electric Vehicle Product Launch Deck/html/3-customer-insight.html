03 — Customer Insight
Three unmet needs behind one purchase decision
Source basis · 1 240 quantitative respondents + 36 in-home interviews, 6 tier-1/2 cities
01
Range they can trust
Buyers discount every published range figure by roughly a third. They budget the trip on winter, loaded, highway numbers — not the brochure.
02
Software that ages up
The last car felt obsolete in 18 months. They now ask what the vehicle will gain after purchase, and whether hardware can carry it.
03
A cabin for three rows of life
Child seat, elderly parent, dog, weekend gear. Interior flexibility outranks material luxury in stated trade-off exercises.
