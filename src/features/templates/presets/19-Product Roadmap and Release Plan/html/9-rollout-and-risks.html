How we validate before we commit
Rollout ladder · every release walks these four gates
Design partners 8 accounts · week 1–2
Weekly build reviews. Gate: 6 of 8 complete the task unassisted.
Private beta 120 accounts · week 3–6
Feature-flagged cohort. Gate: run success ≥ 98.5%, CSAT ≥ 4.2.
Staged GA 10% → 50% → 100% · week 7–9
Automatic rollback on error-rate regression above 0.5 points.
Learn & re-plan week 10
Written review against the exit metric; backlog re-scored before the next train.
Early signal: design-partner pilot cut setup time to 9.4 days across 8 accounts.
Top risks & mitigations
Scheduler rewrite slips HIGH
Blocks R2 approvals and all Phase 2 observability work.
→ Two platform engineers ring-fenced; weekly burn-up to VP Eng.
Templates miss real processes MEDIUM
Generic templates would not move the activation number.
→ Each template sourced from a real customer workflow, then tested.
SOC 2 audit timing MEDIUM
An audit slip pushes enterprise readiness past the FY close.
→ Evidence collection starts Jan ’27, one quarter ahead of the window.
Support load from new steps LOW
→ Enablement kit and macros shipped one week before each GA.
