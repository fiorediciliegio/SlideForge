02 — INTRODUCTION & DESIGN APPROACH
Design is a decision practice, not a decoration layer.
Six years designing money, health and logistics products — the kind where a wrong tap costs someone real time or real euros. I work close to engineering, ship in small increments, and defend every screen with evidence rather than taste.

01
Start with the friction, not the feature
I map where users stall before proposing a screen. The brief is a hypothesis; the drop-off funnel is the truth.
02
Systems over screens
Every component I ship carries states, tokens and accessibility rules, so the next team ships faster without me.
03
Prove it, then scale it
Nothing rolls out to 100% before a measured pilot. Design decisions live or die on task success and support volume.
