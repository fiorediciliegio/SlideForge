04 — RESEARCH & INSIGHTS
People weren't confused by the form. They were afraid of the paperwork.
METHOD MIX
Moderated usability tests14
Support call reviews120
Branch staff interviews6
Funnel & session analytics3 mo
INSIGHT 01
No idea how long it would take
11 of 14 participants expected "a few minutes" and quit when a document upload appeared without warning.
→ Show total effort up front
INSIGHT 02
Documents were never at hand
62% of drop-offs happened within 40 seconds of the ID step — people left to find a passport and never came back.
→ Let them pause and resume
INSIGHT 03
Legal wording read as a warning
Tax residency copy made 8 participants ask whether they were being investigated. Trust collapsed at step 4.
→ Explain why, in plain German
INSIGHT 04
Errors arrived too late
Validation ran only on submit, so a mistyped IBAN sent users back three screens with all fields cleared.
→ Validate inline, keep state
"I stopped because I didn't know if I was two minutes from finishing or twenty."
