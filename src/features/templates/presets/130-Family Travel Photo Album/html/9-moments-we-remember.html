The Part That Stayed

Moments We Will Remember

July 11 · 10:20 p.m. · Wahweap campground, no flashlights

WHAT SURPRISED US

Mia counted eleven shooting stars and made us verify each one. We stayed out an hour longer than any of us planned.

WHAT WE LEARNED

The days we planned least turned out best. Two big things a day was plenty; three made everyone short-tempered.

WHAT WE'LL KEEP

Eleven days with no screens in the car. Mia asked questions we couldn't answer, and we liked not knowing together.

We drove 1,860 miles and the thing we talk about most is lying still on a blanket.
