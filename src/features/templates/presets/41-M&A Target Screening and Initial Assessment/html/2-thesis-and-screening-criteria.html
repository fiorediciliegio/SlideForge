02  ·  STRATEGIC CONTEXT & SCREENING CRITERIA
One thesis drives the screen: buy adjacent capability and recurring revenue — not scale.
ACQUISITION THESIS
01
Why now
Core-market growth decelerating; building the missing capability organically takes 3+ years.
02
What we buy
Adjacent capability, installed base, and contracted recurring revenue.
03
Where we will not go
No operational turnarounds, no customer concentration above 30%, no multi-entity carve-outs.
04
What good looks like
EV $150–600m · EBITDA margin ≥15% · recurring revenue ≥60% · integration owner nameable on day one.
Assumption: thresholds set by Corporate Development with CFO sign-off; weights illustrative for this screen.
