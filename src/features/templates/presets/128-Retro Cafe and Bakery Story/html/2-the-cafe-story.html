The Cafe Story
— how a corner became a kitchen —
… founded on the morning we discovered the best croissant of our lives …

Where it started.

The Hearth Room began the way most small good things do — as a single homemade bread, baked at sunrise for the people who lived on the same street.
Origin

1A corner shop with a wood-burning oven.

It started with a converted grocer's stall on a quiet neighborhood corner. Two friends set up a small wooden counter, hung a chalkboard menu, and began pulling espresso shots by hand at half past six each morning.

"We wanted a place to come back to."
Inspiration

2A feeling, not a format.

Every detail in the room — the warm cream walls, the checked tablecloths, the radio that quietly hums old jazz — was chosen to slow the morning down by a few minutes. The menu is small on purpose, so nothing on it is rushed.

"Slow bread. Loud coffee. Quiet room."
Values

3Three things we never compromise.

① Grain we can name. ② Roast we can date. ③ Hands we can thank. The list of suppliers is printed on the back of every menu card, because the people behind the menu are as much a part of the room as the bread.

"Small kitchen, honest shelf, real names."
