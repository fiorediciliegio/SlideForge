★ Chapter Seven · The Listening Room ★
…a corner of the cafe, mid-morning, when the second regular has just pushed the door closed behind them…
SPACE & EVERYDAY ATMOSPHERE

The room, the morning light.

01
A Sight

Sun through checked curtains.

Late-morning light slants across the marble tables, drawing long warm stripes on the wooden floor. A row of small potted plants lines the window.

02
A Sound

The slow radio at low volume.

An old radio plays a quiet jazz record from 9 to 11 each morning. The only sound above it is the burr of the grinder and the clatter of cups being set down.

03
A Scent

Warm butter meets first brew.

The first just-out-of-the-oven boulangerie carries. Five minutes later the crema of the first espresso joins it. The room never smells of either alone.

04
A Touch

Warm cups, soft napkins.

Cups are heavier than they look — stoneware, never thin porcelain. Linen napkins are folded once and held with a small wooden peg.

05
A Moment

The quiet between orders.

A minute after the rush, a small pause. A kettle ticks. Someone laughs at a table. Outside, a bicycle bell. The room exhales for ten seconds.
