A customer'sday.

Follow Mira — a regular from the second flat up — through one morning at the cafe. Same door, same stool she always takes, same order she always remembers to change.

7:16 AM
Push through the door

The bell gives one short ring. Mira steps out of the morning rain, hangs her coat on the second peg by the wall — never the first.

"the second peg
is mine by habit."
02
7:22 AM
Order at the counter

A laminated menu she does not need. A honey-lavender flat white and a butter croissant today — switch to the rye and the cortado on Wednesdays.

"I always vary
only one of the two."
03
7:25 AM
Find her stool

The stool by the window with the chipped foot — a small flaw the cafe has refused to fix for sentimental reasons. She pads it with a folded napkin.

"the chip is part
of the stool now."
04
7:35 AM
Tear, bite, read

The croissant gives way in three clean layers. She reads the same paperback she has been reading since spring, slowly, with a thumb holding the page.

"three bites,
three pages."
05
8:10 AM
Stay ten more minutes

A second short coffee, ordered at the table by a waiter who knows the table she is at. No menu is brought; the small second cup comes quietly.

"the second cup
is unspoken."
06
8:24 AM
A loaf to take home

On the way out she swaps a paper-wrapped sourdough from the counter for tomorrow's breakfast. The bag is stamped with her name in soft pencil.

"tomorrow,
the same door."
