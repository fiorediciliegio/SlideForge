Dough & Prep

Cold butter is cubed by hand. The overnight dough is folded and rested; rye is shaped into rounds.

06:45

Ovens Warm

The wood oven climbs to baking heat. A kettle is filled. The radio is switched on, low.

07:10

Coffee Breathed

Beans are weighed, ground, and the first espresso is pulled — tasted, and pulled again.

07:25

Counter Set

Pastries are laid out. Napkins are folded. Salt and sugar bowls refilled. The chalk menu is updated.

07:30

Door Unlocked

The bell rings once. The first regular steps in. The day begins for real.
