01  ·  Client context

Meridian has grown volume for four years — and lost margin in every one of them.

Meridian is a $2.4B manufacturer of precision components for the energy and mobility sectors, operating nine plants across three regions. Volume is up 18% since FY23, but each point of growth has been sold at a lower margin than the last.

Three forces behind the decline

List prices rose 3.1% while alloy, energy and freight inputs rose 11.4%.
The plant network runs at 71% utilisation, with three sites below 60%.
The top 40 accounts — 58% of revenue — are renegotiated locally, without margin guardrails.
