03  ·  BRAND PLATFORM
“The house you wish your friend in Charleston lived in.”
BRAND PROMISE, INTERNAL
A residential brand, not a hotel brand
Four pillars govern every design, hiring, and pricing decision. Anything that fails a pillar does not get built.
I.
Rooted, not themed
Lowcountry materials, local makers, zero pastiche. 82% of FF&E sourced within 300 miles.
II.
Hosted, not serviced
No front desk. Nine cross-trained hosts own arrival, bar, and neighbourhood planning end to end.
III.
Locals first, guests always
The courtyard bar underwrites itself on resident traffic — 45% of covers from outside the house.
IV.
Direct by design
Owned channel and repeat stays carry the mix; OTA capped at 22% of room nights at stabilisation.
