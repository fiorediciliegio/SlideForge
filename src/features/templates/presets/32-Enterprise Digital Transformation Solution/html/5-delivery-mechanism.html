04  /  Delivery mechanism
A repeatable 10-week loop, run by product teams
Every use case moves through the same five stages, so estimating and staffing become predictable by Wave 2.
STAGE 01
Frame
Value hypothesis, baseline metric and named business owner agreed in writing.
Weeks 1–2
STAGE 02
Wire the data
Source data promoted into a governed data product with quality thresholds.
Weeks 2–5
STAGE 03
Build thin slice
One journey, one site, real users. Reusable API contracts published, not one-offs.
Weeks 4–8
STAGE 04
Prove & decide
Measured against baseline. Scale, adjust or stop — decided at the value board.
Weeks 8–10
STAGE 05
Scale & embed
Rolled out by site, with adoption targets and run ownership handed to the function.
Continuous
Three rules keep the loop honest
They are the difference between a pilot portfolio and a transformation.
10wk
No initiative runs longer without a decision
Anything that cannot show a measured result in one loop is re-scoped or stopped.
1
One accountable business owner
Named in the business case, holds the benefit in their own operating plan.
0
New point-to-point interfaces
Every integration ships as a published, reusable contract on the backbone.
Team shape per loop: 1 product owner, 1 data engineer, 2 developers, 0.5 architect, 0.5 change lead
