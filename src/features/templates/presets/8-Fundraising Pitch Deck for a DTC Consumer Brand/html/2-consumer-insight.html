01 — Consumer insight
The routine got longer. The confidence got shorter.
Premium skincare shoppers are buying more products than ever and trusting them less. The bottleneck is no longer access or price — it is verifiable proof and fewer decisions.
01 Routines have grown to 6+ steps, yet satisfaction with results has fallen.
02 Ingredient claims are the top source of distrust, ahead of price.
03 Refill and subscription formats are now expected, not a novelty.
68% of premium skincare discovery now begins in social video — not on a shelf.
3 in 5 shoppers abandoned a routine in the last year because results felt unclear.
41% say they cannot tell which active ingredient actually did the work.
Why now
Creator-led proof is cheap to distribute
Clinical testing costs fell ~40% since 2022
Refill supply chains finally scale under $5/unit
