Four lessons, each with a change we are making
# What we learned Operating change for Q3 Evidence it worked Owner · live from
01
Capacity must be sized before a key result is signed
70% plan coverage needed roughly twice the hours the team had.
 Every proposed key result carries a one-line capacity estimate. Anything above 80% of available hours is cut or re-scoped at planning. No Q3 key result exceeds 75% of owner capacity A. Reyes
Wk 1
02
Do not own a result another team delivers
Tracking coverage sat behind a queue we could not prioritise.
 Cross-team key results require a named counterpart and a written commitment before the quarter opens, or they become a stretch item. Data eng. sprint capacity confirmed for weeks 1–6 Sam O.
Wk 1
03
In-product changes beat manual motions
Both wins came from shipped product, not added effort.
 Each objective must name at least one in-product lever. Motions that only add human hours need an explicit justification. All three Q3 objectives carry a product lever Priya N.
Wk 2
04
Four objectives split attention too thin
The two lowest-weighted objectives got the least review time.
 Three objectives maximum, each reviewed in a 30-minute weekly slot with the metric on screen and a written call. Q3 scope reduced to 3 objectives and 9 key results A. Reyes
