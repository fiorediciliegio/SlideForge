Eight small routines that survive a busy term
None of this requires a perfect week. Pick the two that feel easiest and let the rest follow.
Sleep rhythmKeep bed and wake times roughly similar, even after one late night.
Balanced platesA protein, a grain and something colourful. No food needs to be banned.
Gentle hygieneWarm water or mild soap outside only. Douching tends to irritate rather than help.
Medicine routineTake prescribed medicine as directed. Ask a clinician before any change.
Water nearbyHeadaches and fuzzy focus often travel with not drinking enough.
Movement you likeWalking between classes counts. Enjoyment makes it repeatable.
One stress resetMusic, slow breathing or a short walk — used before you hit your limit.
Someone to tellKeep one regular check-in with a friend. Isolation makes everything heavier.
These habits support general well-being. They are not treatments — and detoxes, cleanses or restrictive diets are not part of the plan.
