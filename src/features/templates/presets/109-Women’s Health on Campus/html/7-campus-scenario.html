Maya’s week: from pushing
through to booking in
An illustrative story, not a case study. Maya is a composite student — her steps, not her symptoms, are the lesson.
STEP 1 · WEEK ONECramps arrive in a 9am seminar. Maya takes what usually helps and keeps going. Nothing unusual yet.
STEP 2 · WEEK THREEThree cycles running now, and two missed seminars. She writes the dates down instead of searching symptoms.
STEP 3 · THE SAME WEEKShe books at the campus clinic, brings her notes, and asks what her options are. No drama — just a plan.
What Maya did wellShe waited for a pattern rather than panicking on day one, kept a simple record, and asked while it was still a routine appointment.
If it had been urgentSudden severe pain or heavy bleeding changes the plan: seek same-day care.
