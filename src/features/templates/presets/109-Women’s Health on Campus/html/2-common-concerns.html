What students actually carry with them
Seven areas that come up most often in student health services — each one common, manageable, and nothing to be embarrassed about.
01 · Menstrual healthCycle length, flow and cramping vary widely between people, and can shift with stress or illness.
02 · Sexual & reproductiveContraception, screening, vaccination and consent. Confidential services exist for people who feel well.
03 · SleepIrregular nights cluster around deadlines and shared living, and they influence focus and mood.
04 · Stress & moodDeadline pressure, being far from home and constant comparison build up quietly across a term.
05 · Eating & energySkipped meals, tight budgets and canteen hours shape what is realistic. Regular beats perfect.
06 · Body changesSkin, weight, hair and breast tissue often keep changing in early adulthood. Know your own normal.
07 · Access to careA new city, an unfamiliar system, registration and insurance questions often delay care the most.
Health needs differ from person to person. Not everyone menstruates or can become pregnant, and anatomy, culture and history all shape what care looks like — treat this as a map, not a checklist about you.
