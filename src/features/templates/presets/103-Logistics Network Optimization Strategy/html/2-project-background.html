The network was built for a business
we no longer run.

The current footprint was sized in 2018 around a wholesale, pallet-out business. Since then order volume shifted to small parcel and store-direct, and demand migrated south and west — but not a single stocking location moved.

Reduce logistics cost-to-serve to under 7% of revenue by FY28 without degrading service — and prove it with a modelled, phased plan rather than a cost-cutting target.
