Quick Recap
4 Rules
To Remember
Keep
It Simple!
01
Push

A force that moves something away or acts in the same direction as your push.

02
Pull

A force that moves something toward you or acts in the pull direction.

03
Gravity

A force that pulls masses toward each other; near Earth it acts downward.

04
Friction

A force that opposes sliding motion between surfaces in contact.

Checklist
