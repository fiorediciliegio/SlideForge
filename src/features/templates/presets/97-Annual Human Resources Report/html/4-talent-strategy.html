The FY2026 talent architecture

One ambition, three operating pillars, one measurement layer. Every people programme this year was funded only if it laddered into this structure.

Ambition

Build capability faster than headcount — grow skills 2× faster than we grow people

Three university and returner pipelines for scarce engineering skills.

Quarterly listening cycle replacing the single annual survey.
