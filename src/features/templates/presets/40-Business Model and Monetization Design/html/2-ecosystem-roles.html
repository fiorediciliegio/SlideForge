02  ·  Customer problem & ecosystem roles

The buyer's problem is not finding capacity.
It is trusting it.

SO THE PLATFORM'S JOB IS

To absorb counterparty risk on both sides — verification, escrow and a single contract — so each side exchanges something it could not exchange directly.

DEMAND MUST GAIN

A shorter path from need to accepted work — at a landed cost below its incumbent premium.

SUPPLY MUST GAIN

Utilisation it cannot source alone, paid on time — worth more than the fee it gives up.

THE PLATFORM MUST GAIN

A record of every cleared transaction — the asset that later prices risk and sells data.
