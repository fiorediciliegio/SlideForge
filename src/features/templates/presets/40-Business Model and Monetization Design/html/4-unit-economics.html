ANNOTATED ASSUMPTIONS

Basket $6,000, take rate 7%. Revenue per transaction is a function of basket size, so mid-market baskets are the design target.

Verification is 55% manual today. Automating review to 20% manual moves contribution from 38% to roughly 49% with no price change.

Payments are a pass-through floor. Escrow and card cost scales with value cleared and will not improve until volume earns rate cards.

BREAK-EVEN SCALE

Fixed base of $310k per month must be covered by contribution.

Break-even ≈ 1,930 cleared transactions per month, or $11.6m of value cleared. Automating verification pulls that to ≈ 1,490.
