VALIDATION EXPERIMENTS — NEXT TWO QUARTERS

 H EXPERIMENT KILL / PASS SIGNAL
H1 Priced pilot with 12 buyers at 7% versus a 4% control cohort, same verification depth. Pass if repeat rate at 7% is within 5pts of control.
H2 Offer guaranteed settlement to 40 providers; measure listed capacity and fee acceptance. Kill if under half list capacity within 30 days.
H3 Shadow-run automated review against human reviewers on 500 historical jobs. Pass if agreement ≥ 95% with no missed severe defect.
H4 Gate the workflow record behind seats for new accounts only; watch churn and attach. Pass if attach ≥ 30% without transaction decline.
