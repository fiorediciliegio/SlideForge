Small Changes, Big Effects

What Makes
A Reaction
Go Wild?

Reactions can suddenly speed up, foam, change color or release gas.
It all comes down to a few key factors at the microscopic level.

Same atoms.
Different outcomes.
01
ParticlesMore collisions, faster reactions
When particles move faster or are more concentrated, they collide more often, increasing the chance of a successful reaction.
02
EnergyOvercome the energy barrier
Adding heat or light gives particles more energy, helping them overcome the activation energy and react more quickly.
03
ContactGreater surface, faster change
Breaking solids into smaller pieces increases the surface area, allowing more particles to come into contact and react.
04
ConditionsThe right environment matters
Changes in temperature, pressure, acidity (pH) or the presence of a catalyst can dramatically affect the reaction rate and outcome.
