TRY IT YOURSELF!
You need:
🎈 1 balloon  •  🧑 your head  •  📄 paper bits
1
BLOW IT UP!
Blow up a balloon and tie the end. Ask a grown-up if you need a hand.
2
RUB!
RUB ON HAIR!
Rub the balloon on your head, back and forth, about 10 times.
3
HOVER OVER PAPER!
Tear tiny paper bits. Hold the balloon just above them.
4
WOW!
WATCH THEM JUMP!
The paper bits leap up and stick to the balloon like magic!
👀 WHAT'S HAPPENING: The balloon steals tiny electrons from your hair. Now it has extra – charge, so it pulls the + side of the paper right up to it!
🛡 SAFETY TIP!
Works BEST on a dry day. Damp air stops the trick — try again when it's not raining!
