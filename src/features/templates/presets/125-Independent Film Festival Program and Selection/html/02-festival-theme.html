The

Theme
II
Thesis

The Crossing asks what lives at the seam — between countries, generations, languages, and the private rooms we keep from ourselves. This year's eleven films are united not by geography but by motion; each carries a character, an image, or a season that no longer fits where it began.

Cultural Moment

Independent film has always looked sideways at the official story. In a year marked by closed borders and reopened cities, the festival gathers artists who refuse the easy narration — who hold the camera steady across a longer breath, and trust the audience to follow.

Anchor Note

"A border is not a line — it is the moment a life decides to keep moving."
