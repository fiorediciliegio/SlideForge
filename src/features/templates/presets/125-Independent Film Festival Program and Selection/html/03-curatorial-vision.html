On Selection
We choose
films that
refuse the
easy cut.

Meridian prefers the long take and the unanswered question. We champion first and second features that take a real risk with form — and directors whose earlier work has already proven they are not chasing applause, but conversation.

Four Principles of Selection
01
Authorial Voice

A director whose voice is unborrowed — identifiable from the first frame, without a credit. We look for a hand, not a genre.

02
Structural Risk

Films that experiment with time, frame, and silence — works that trust the image to hold meaning more than the line.

03
Cinematic Independence

Produced outside the dominant studio system, financed by national funds, family, or international co-production credits we can trace.

04
The 70% Threshold

At least 70% of the program is led by women, non-binary, or first-time directors — measured publicly in the appendix.
