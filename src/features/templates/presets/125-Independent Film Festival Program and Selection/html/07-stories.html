Films 06 · 11
A Note on Subject

No two films in this year's program address the same subject. The threads overlap deliberately — the same body, the same door, or the same letter, can reappear in a documentary as easily as in a fiction. The eleven films are not a survey; they are a conversation that you, the audience, will overhear in five rooms at once.
