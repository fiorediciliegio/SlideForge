For Audiences · Three Suggested Routes
A Reader's Map
How to listen
to the program.

We propose three listening notes — like a program handed to you at the door. None is the right one. Choose the route that lets the festival keep its own pace for you. Each route reserves time after film for a quiet, public conversation.

I.
Route
The Arrivals

If it is your first festival — begin with F-04 (Drift) and F-05 (Quiet North) on Day 02. Both are generous, dialogue-led, and short enough to leave room for the conversation that follows. Return on Day 03 for F-07 (Surname).

Best for — first-time festival-goers
II.
Route
The Long Walk

If you have been here before — pair F-01 (Halftones, opening night) with F-11 (Slow Crossing, closing) and walk between them on the next four days. The two bookends were chosen to rhyme — let the festival do the work.

Best for — Meridian regulars
Special Session
III. Quiet Round

Three Saturday-morning screenings in L'Atelier with the lights at half — coffee, no phones, post-film conversation hosted by the programmer. A free add-on at the box office.
