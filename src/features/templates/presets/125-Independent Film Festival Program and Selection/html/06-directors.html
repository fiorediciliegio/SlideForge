Brief editorial profiles of four film-makers whose work defines the cross-section of voices in this year's program.

D · 01
Hungary / Austria · Film 01
M. Eberhardt

Je n'appartiens plus ici

Trained as a documentary editor, Eberhardt turns to fiction with the patience of an archivist and the eye of someone who has held a stranger's passport.

Approach — slow, evidentiary, hand-made 16mm.
D · 02
Mexico · Film 02
A. Rivera-Calleja

La frontera que se canta

A former theatre director, Rivera-Calleja gathers non-professional performers from the border towns she films and writes in the second person, never in the third.

Approach — community casting, long rehearsal.
D · 03
Türkiye · Film 03
S. Karadağ

A second returning

One of the first documentary directors from the Black Sea coast to map the gentrification of their own village in 4K, soundtracked by the failing gulls of the harbour.

Approach — observed, unhurried, lyrical.
D · 04
South Korea · Film 04
L. Chen · Y. Park

Drift — shared authorship

A sibling duo whose first collaborative feature reframes the family apartment as a linguistic territory — a co-direction that resists the singular authorial voice.

Approach — co-authored, dialogic, indoor.
