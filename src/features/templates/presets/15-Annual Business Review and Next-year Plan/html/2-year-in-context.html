The year in context
Entering FY2025 with growth
slowing and margin under pressure
We came into the year growing fast but burning cash — and the market stopped paying for growth alone.
Budgets tightened, procurement added security and ROI gates, and our own cost base had grown faster than revenue for two straight years. FY2025 was framed as a reset year: prove that the core business can compound and pay for itself.
Board mandate, Jan 2025: grow above 20%, exit the year EBITDA positive, and do not trade away retention.
