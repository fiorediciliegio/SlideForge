NOVASCALE  ·  EMPLOYER BRAND PROGRAM

04  /  Solution overview

One promise, four provable pillars

Mission and talent vision translated into an employer value proposition every recruiter, hiring manager and employee can repeat.

Mission

Make large-scale compute simple enough that any team can build with it.

Talent vision

A team of 1,200 builders who would each be the strongest hire on any other team.

Brand line

Built in the open.

01

Hard problems, early

Engineers touch production scheduling, storage and inference systems in their first quarter.

02

Small teams, real ownership

Teams of six to eight own a service end to end, from design memo to on-call rotation.

03

Growth you can see

A published engineering ladder, twice-yearly calibration and a funded learning budget.

04

Work that stays public

Design docs, postmortems and open-source patches published under the engineering blog.

Rule: no pillar ships without a named proof point — a person, a number or an artefact.
