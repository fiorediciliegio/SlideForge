05  /  Culture & ways of working

How a decision travels here

Culture is a mechanism, not a mood. This is the loop every team runs — and the loop candidates are shown in interviews.

Step 01

Write it down

A one-page memo before any meeting is booked.

→

Step 02

Argue in the open

Comments stay public; the loudest voice holds no veto.

→

Step 03

Name one owner

Every decision carries a single accountable engineer.

→

Step 04

Ship in a week

Smallest useful slice reaches production first.

→

Step 05

Publish the learning

Blameless postmortems go out to the whole company.

The rituals that keep the loop honest

Demo Friday  ·  Working software only — 12 minutes, no slides, every team every week.

Focus blocks  ·  No internal meetings before 13:00; on-call and incidents excepted.

Rotation week  ·  Each engineer spends one week a year embedded with a customer team.
