02  /  Buyer insight

The owner does not lack software. She lacks a team to land it.

Primary buyer

Owner-operator, 25–150 staff, no CIO

Signs the cheque herself. Judges vendors on how fast something visibly works, not on architecture. One failed rollout ends the category for two years.

“I bought the licences in March. In September nobody was using them, so I cancelled.”

— Operations director, 60-seat logistics firm

Why deals stall — 212 lost-deal reviews, 2025–26

Four of the top five blockers are delivery, not product

No internal owner for the rollout 58%
Data migration seen as too risky 47%
Quote arrives without a fixed timeline 41%
Staff training left to the customer 36%
Missing product capability 12%

Implication for both firms

Whoever removes the delivery risk wins the account — the product comparison never happens.
