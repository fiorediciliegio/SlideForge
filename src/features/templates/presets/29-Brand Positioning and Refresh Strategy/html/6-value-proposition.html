05 — Brand platform
One idea, carried down
to every proof point
The platform is deliberately narrow. Anything that cannot be traced up to the idea does not get made, briefed or funded.
Core idea
Care you can prove — and still call your own.
Pillar 01
Open Formula
Every ingredient, dose and supplier published on pack and online — no proprietary blends, no hidden percentages.
Proof: full INCI disclosure across 100% of SKUs by Q3
Pillar 02
Earned Result
Claims tied to published clinical panels, with honest timelines instead of overnight-transformation language.
Proof: 12-week third-party efficacy study on the hero range
Pillar 03
Your Ritual
Routine-building tools and refill architecture that let people adapt the range rather than follow a prescription.
Proof: refill system + routine planner launching with Wave 2
