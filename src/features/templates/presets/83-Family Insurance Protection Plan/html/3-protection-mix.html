Supplemental — refine the edges
Child rider, waiver of premium, accidental death — added last.
Reviewed at each annual check-in
Core — keep income flowing
Critical illness lump sum plus disability income to age 65. Pays when the earner survives but cannot work.
Addresses 45% of the identified gap
Foundation — replace the paycheck
Level term to age 60, laddered across both earners. Covers the mortgage, living costs and the education fund in one benefit.
Sized at 10× household income
