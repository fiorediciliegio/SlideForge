04 — Operating mechanism
How repricing actually reaches the P&L
A five-stage gate with a named owner and a hard exit criterion at each step. Reviewed fortnightly by the Executive Committee.
Stage 01
Segment the book
Score all 396 contracts on renewal date, switching cost and volume dependency.
Exit — tiered target list
Stage 02
Set the index clause
Standard alloy-and-freight pass-through, quarterly reset, 2% collar.
Exit — legal template signed
Stage 03
Negotiate at renewal
Account teams carry a defined walk-away floor; escalation to CCO above 3%.
Exit — signed amendment
Stage 04
Protect the volume
Pair every increase with a service or lead-time commitment to hold retention.
Exit — churn under 4%
Stage 05
Land in the margin
Realised price tracked monthly against index; variance reported to the board.
Exit — +110 bps run-rate
Governance & cadence
Executive Committee
Fortnightly · owns the target list and walk-away floors
Pricing Council
Weekly · CFO chair, resolves exceptions within 5 days
Board reporting
Quarterly · realised price index and retention variance
Trigger to pause
Churn above 6% in any two consecutive months
