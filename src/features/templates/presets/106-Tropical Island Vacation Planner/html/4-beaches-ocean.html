04 · BEACHES & OCEAN EXPERIENCES

Pick the water to
match the hour

Four shore settings, four different reasons to get wet. Check local conditions and signage on the day.

◐ SOUTH COVE · CALM SWIM

Sheltered, shallow entry and shade close to the sand — the default early-morning swim with younger or nervous swimmers.

◐ WEST LAGOON · PADDLE & SAIL

Flat water for paddleboards and small sailing boats. Ask your stay about a half-day rental if the wind allows.

◐ REEF POINT · SNORKEL

Go with a guided boat trip rather than swimming out. Bring your own mask; rentals may be limited.

◐ NORTH SHORE · SUNSET

Bigger swell, better light. Watch from the sand instead of swimming late in the day.
