05 · NATURE BEYOND THE BEACH

Walk inland when
the sand gets hot

The interior is the shade option: forest trails, falling water and high viewpoints, all reachable from the coastal road.

▲ RIDGE TRAIL & VIEWPOINT

A short climb to see the whole coastline at once. Best in the first hours after sunrise; carry water and check trail status locally.

▲ FRESHWATER FALLS POOL

Cool, non-salt swimming under the cliff. Footing can be slick — bring shoes you can walk in wet.

▲ BOTANICAL GARDEN & BIRDS

Flat, easy paths through planted tropical species with resident birdlife — the low-effort option.

PROTECTED AREA NOTE

Some inland sites sit inside reserves. Confirm access, fees and guide requirements before the day.
