06 · WHERE TO STAY

Your base decides how the days feel

Three positions along the same coast. Compare access and atmosphere — we deliberately avoid star ratings and review scores we cannot verify.

⌂ A · BEACHFRONT BUNGALOW

Steps to the sand, quietest at night, fewest dining options nearby.

⌂ B · POOL GUESTHOUSE

Shade and a pool for midday, short walk down to the water.

⌂ C · TOWN APARTMENT

Food and transport at the door, more street noise, no beach view.

CHOOSE BY
PRIORITY

Early swims → Zone A. You will want a car or scooter.

Kids or long naps → Zone B. Pool access matters more than sand.

No vehicle → Zone C. Walk to meals, ride out to beaches.
