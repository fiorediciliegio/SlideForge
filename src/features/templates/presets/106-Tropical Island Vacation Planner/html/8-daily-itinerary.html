08 · RELAXED DAILY ITINERARY

One outing a day, then nothing

PACING RULE

Nothing scheduled between 12:00 and 15:00. Times are intentions, not bookings.

1

DAY 1 · ARRIVE & SETTLE

PM — Ferry arrival, transfer to your stay, unpack.

Late PM — Short walk to the nearest sand, feet in the water only.

Evening — Simple meal near the room, early night.

EASY · TRANSFER [XX] MIN — CONFIRM LOCALLY

2

DAY 2 · WATER DAY

07:30 — Calm-cove swim before the heat.

10:00 — Guided reef snorkel trip.

12:00–15:00 — Lunch, shade, nap.

17:30 — Harbour dinner, sunset from the sand.

ACTIVE · BOOK THE BOAT ON DAY 1

3

DAY 3 · GREEN DAY

06:45 — Ridge trail while it is cool.

09:30 — Freshwater falls swim, slow breakfast after.

Midday — Back to the room; no plans.

16:30 — Garden paths, then market dessert walk.

MODERATE · WATER + SHOES YOU CAN WET

4

DAY 4 · SLOW & DEPART

AM — Last swim, pack with time to spare.

Midday — Fruit lunch, check out, leave for the pier early.

Buffer — Add one spare hour before onward travel.

EASY · AVOID A TIGHT ONWARD CONNECTION

STAYING LONGER?

Repeat the Day 2 shape for extra days and swap in the paddle-and-sail morning or a second beach — keep one empty day per week.
