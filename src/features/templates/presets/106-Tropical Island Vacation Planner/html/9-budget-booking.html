WHERE THE EASY SAVINGS ARE

Travel on quieter mid-week dates and shift one hotel night to a cheaper zone.

Buy fruit and water at the market instead of the resort shop.

Bring your own snorkel gear and reusable bottles to avoid daily rentals.

BOOK IN THIS ORDER

1 · ARRIVAL LEG
Lock flights and the island transfer together — a cheap flight that misses the last boat costs more.

2 · ROOM FOR EVERY NIGHT
Prefer free-cancellation rates while dates may still move.

3 · ONE GUIDED TRIP
Reserve the boat outing after you arrive, once you can read the weather.

4 · LOCAL TRANSPORT
Compare a rental for the whole stay against paying per ride.
