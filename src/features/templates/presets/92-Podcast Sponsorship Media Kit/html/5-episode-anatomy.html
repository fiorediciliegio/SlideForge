05 — How it works

Anatomy of a host-read integration

Every placement is scripted from a real product session, then read in Maya's voice inside the episode edit.

Placement map — 52-minute episode

Production workflow — 3 weeks from brief to air

Step 01

Brief & fit check

We confirm category exclusivity and agree the single claim worth making.

Step 02

Product session

45-minute walkthrough with your team so the read is first-hand, not a script.

Step 03

Script & approval

You get the written read 5 days before air. One revision round included.

Step 04

Record & mix

Read in-studio and placed in the edit with a unique tracking URL and code.

Step 05

Air & report

Live Tuesday 06:00 ET. Performance report lands on day 14 and day 30.

Assets required from sponsor: one-page brief, offer code, landing URL. Nothing else.
