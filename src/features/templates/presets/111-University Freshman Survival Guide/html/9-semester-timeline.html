Three habits that turn this calendar into a non-event
01
Put all four dates in your phone today
Your registrar publishes them before term starts. One evening of setup buys a calm semester.
02
Set every reminder two weeks early
An alert on the day is not a reminder — it's an announcement that you're already late.
03
Protect the week before each bar
Say no to new commitments in W5, W8 and W12. That's where the semester is actually won.
