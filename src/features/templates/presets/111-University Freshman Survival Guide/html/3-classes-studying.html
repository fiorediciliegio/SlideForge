Classes & Studying Smart
You're not in high school anymore — here's how the game changes.
#1 RULE
📄 Read the syllabus.
Then read it again.
Every deadline, exam date, and grade weight lives in there. Copy them into your calendar on Week 1 — never ask "when is it due?"
HABIT
🎯 Just show up.
80% of the grade.
Sit in the front third of the room. Introduce yourself to the person next to you. Ask one question per lecture — profs remember faces.
SECRET WIN
🚪 Go to office hours.
Almost nobody does.
Show up in Week 2 with one specific question. It builds rapport, unlocks better feedback, and future rec letters.
