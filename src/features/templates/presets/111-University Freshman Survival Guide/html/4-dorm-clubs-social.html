📌 Club Fair Week
Sign up for everything you're curious about. You can quit anything at any time — but you can't join what you don't know exists.
💬 SOCIAL
Making friends 101
💡 Say YES for the first 30 days. Dining hall invites, hallway movie nights, random ping-pong — all of it.

💡 Ask "what's your name again?" twice. Nobody remembers after Welcome Week — everyone is bluffing.

💡 Have a "let's grab food" line ready.
"hey, I'm heading to the dining hall — wanna come?"
🏡 feeling homesick?
It's normal. Weeks 3–5 are the hardest. Call home, but don't go home. The homesick weeks are also the friend-making weeks.
