05 · Treatment and outcome
Treatment, outcome and clinical lessons
Cure is measured in months of adherence and a falling titre — not in defervescence.
Treatment path
Step 1 · Regimen
Doxycycline + hydroxychloroquine
100 mg q12h and 200 mg q8h; alkalinisation makes doxycycline bactericidal.
Step 2 · Duration
18 months, native valve
≥24 months with prosthetic material. Shorter courses carry roughly ten-fold relapse risk.
Step 3 · Safety
Retinal screening every 6 months
Baseline ophthalmology, then 6-monthly. Counsel photoprotection; periodic bloods.
Step 4 · Surgery
Only on standard indications
Heart failure, uncontrolled infection or embolic risk. Managed medically here.
Follow-up — serologic response
Anti-phase I IgG titre by immunofluorescence, on a doubling-dilution scale.
Three clinical lessons
1
A normal echocardiogram does not exclude endocarditis
Vegetations appear on TTE in only about 12% of Q fever cases. Serology carries this diagnosis.
2
Ask the exposure question before the culture returns
Livestock contact plus a known valvulopathy should trigger Coxiella serology on day one.
3
Treatment ends on serology, and follow-up outlives it
Stop on a fourfold fall below 1:200; monitor titres ≥5 years. Three-year mortality ~7% treated.
