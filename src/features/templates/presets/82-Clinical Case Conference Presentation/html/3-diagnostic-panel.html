Pattern to name aloud: anaemia, mild thrombocytopenia, transaminitis and autoantibody positivity with a normal white cell count — the chronic Q fever signature, not pyogenic sepsis.
Panel C — examination
Grade 2/6 early diastolic murmur at the left sternal edge · splenomegaly 3 cm · no Osler nodes, splinter haemorrhages or Roth spots.
Panel B — the diagnosis-making test

1:1024
Threshold >1:800 is a major microbiologic criterion in Duke-ISCVID 2023. Phase II IgG 1:512; whole-blood PCR negative.
Panel D — imaging, read with its limits
Fig. 2 · TEE
Leaflet thickening, no discrete vegetation. Reference image. Vegetations are seen in only ~12% of Q fever cases on TTE — a normal echo excludes nothing.
Fig. 3 · FDG PET/CT
Now a major imaging criterion. Reference image. Native-valve sensitivity is only ~22–31%, so a negative scan cannot reassure.
