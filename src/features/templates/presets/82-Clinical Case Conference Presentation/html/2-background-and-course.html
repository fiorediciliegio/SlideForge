02 · Case narrative
Background and course
Day −70 · Exposure
Assisted lambing on the family farm
No acute febrile illness recognised at the time.
Day 0 · Chief complaint
Six weeks of fever and night sweats
Temperature to 38.6 °C, 7 kg weight loss, profound fatigue.
Day 7 · Interim course
Ten days of oral amoxicillin
Treated as atypical pneumonia; fever recurred on day 19.
Day 21 · Key event
New grade 2/6 diastolic murmur
TTE: bicuspid aortic valve, mild regurgitation, no vegetation.
Day 24 · Initial assessment
Five culture sets negative at 72 h
Referred to the Endocarditis Team as suspected BCNIE.
