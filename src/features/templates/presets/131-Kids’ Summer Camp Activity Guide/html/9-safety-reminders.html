SAFE CAMPERS HAVE MORE FUN
Six Rules We Never Skip
Drink Often
Water break at every stop on the schedule. Refill stations sit at the Rest Grove.
Hat On
Sunscreen at home and again after water play. Hats on outdoors.
Stay With Your Buddy
Move in pairs. If your buddy stops, you stop too.
Yellow Flag Means Stop
Flags mark the edge of camp. Never cross one, even chasing a ball.
Use Gear the Right Way
Helmets and pads on for wheels. Tools stay in the Maker Barn.
Tell a Leader Right Away
Hurt, lost or upset? Find the nearest leader. Kind words only.
