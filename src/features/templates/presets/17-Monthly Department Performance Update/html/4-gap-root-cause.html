04  /  ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS
Why response time slipped
The SLA breach is not a productivity problem. Agents handled 8% more tickets each. The queue simply arrived faster than staffed capacity — and three quarters of the added wait traces to just two causes.
WHAT THE DATA RULES OUT
Tickets per agent per day rose from 24.6 to 26.6 and CSAT held at 4.51. Neither effort nor skill is the constraint.
CONTRIBUTION TO THE 41-MINUTE SLA GAP
Billing-migration ticket spike
18 min  ·  44%
6,400 unplanned tickets from the payments platform cutover in week 2
Two vacancies unfilled
13 min  ·  32%
Tier-2 roles open for 9 and 11 weeks — 320 agent-hours lost
Routing misassignment
6 min  ·  15%
11% of tickets reassigned at least once before first reply
Other & seasonal noise
4 min  ·  9%
Holiday coverage gaps and two short tooling outages
IMPLICATION FOR AUGUST
76% of the gap is addressable this month: the migration backlog clears by 8 Aug and two approved offers start 11 Aug. Routing fixes need engineering time we do not yet hold.
